## What the document says and what the evaluator makes of it

| Document | Evaluator (sim3d.rs unless said) |
|---|---|
| `object3D primitive="voxels"` + `rigidBody` (shape none/auto/voxels, `density`, no `mass`) | `shape_for`: the asset (`voxel_asset::load`) -> `Shape3::Voxels{size: cellSize*scale (per axis), cells}`, `mass = voxels::body(..)` = cells x density x (size/ppm)^3. cellSize: object3D@cellSize, else the asset's, else 1 (the loader's rule). Body origin = the box's minimum corner = the node's origin (Saturno's frame), so `start` is the node's pose as now. |
| + `crater source=..` (CRT5/13-16, static/kinematic or dynamic owner) | `maxFragments` (64) placeholder bodies appended as `Body3Node{fragment:true}` + one `VoxelSplit3{parent, slots}`; `link_craters` makes the watch as now; the driver answers `voxel_cut(t, parent, revision, impact)` with `crater_cut(..)` (conserving kernel, bulking 1; `Rock{size, density, ppm, policy}`: min_cells = fragmentMinCells (1), max_fragments, overflow (error), stay = `anchor` (base: the cells of the greatest y key anchor; largest)). One watch, so one cut, revision 1. |
| + `fracture [source=..] partition=..` (FRX8-12) | at build, `voxels::fracture(asset occupancy, Partition from the attributes, FracturePolicy, size, density, ppm)`; pieces become hidden dynamic fragment bodies (`Fracture3.fragments`, offset 0, `impulse` split by mass), `dust: dust_body`, `at`/`radialImpulse`/contact as for meshes. voronoi: `Partition::Voronoi{seeds: pieces, seed}`; planes: object units -> doubled cell coordinates (`u = 2*cell_coordinate/cellSize` exactly when the plane's offset is a multiple of half a cell, else rounded toward the cell side and said so); labels=material: palette index of the cell. |
| `burst crater=..` of a cell crater (CRT16, no count) | one particle per thrown cell of the same memoized cut (position, velocity, palette colour, mass of a cell): the cut is a pure function of the impact, so the world's driver and the particle system compute it once (memo like `CraterSource`'s). |

Everything is done in object units with the node's scale (VOX13: uniform when a crater or a fracture breaks it): the kernel is read with `kernel_unit = scale/ppm` metres per object unit and the cells' `h = cellSize*scale/ppm`; the impact point goes to the lattice by `object_to_cell`'s inverse of the node's world matrix, as `CraterSource` does with `impact.point / scale`.

## Stages (each a commit series on this branch, red first)

1. **W1 body.** Oracles: (a) a document with a voxel block and a ball, gravity 0, the ball hits the block head on with restitution 0 and friction 0: the common velocity gives the block's mass, which is N x density x cell volume to 1e-6 (end to end through `Evaluator`); (b) the built world's body has the shape's exact mass properties (the `hulls` and the spec's mass agree); (c) a voxel body with a non-uniform scale and no crater/fracture is allowed and its cells are scaled per axis; (d) a missing asset, a non-cell shape with `density`, `mass` with cells are refused by the rules (already) and by the evaluator with the node's id if they slip through; (e) a document with no voxel object has the same world identity digest as before (golden values of two existing documents, and `digest_world3` unchanged for them).
2. **W2 cut.** Oracles: a ground of cells (a `.srvol` that the test writes: 120 x 40 x 120 cells of 0.25 m) with a crater and a ball of the law's mass and speed: the world's frame after the impact says `voxel_revision` Some(1), `voxel_cells_at(ground, 1)` = the cells of the asset less the removed plus the rim (counts PINNED from the run, in the style of 6 460 / 1 292, and checked against `crater_cut` called on the same impact: the document and the pure function agree), a piece takes a slot when the cut leaves a loose part, nothing is cut before the impact, the frame is the same four ways (the helper of voxel_split.rs with a document world), a second impact is not a second cut (CRT15: one watch).
3. **W3 fracture.** Oracles: a document with a block and `fracture` by seeds/planes/labels: the fragments' cells + dust = the asset's cells, masses sum to the source's, the world fires at `at` and fragments + `fracture_lost` carry the source's momentum (1e-9), `fragmentMinCells` makes dust, `fragmentOverflow=error` is an evaluator failure naming both numbers; a fracture on a mesh is untouched (its tests unchanged).
4. **W4 out.** `Evaluator` gives the renderer, per frame and voxel body, the revision and an immutable `Arc<Occupancy>` (cells of `voxel_cells_at` with the asset's palette index per cell, and the rim's from the excavation), memoized by (body, revision); the burst of a cell crater births its thrown cells. Oracles: the grid at revision 0 equals the asset's occupancy (fingerprint), at revision 1 equals asset - removed + rim with the palette indices of each; asking a frame twice does not rebuild it (count); the births of a burst are the thrown cells in the order of the cut with the cut's speeds.
5. **W5 identity and limits.** Every scene without voxels bit-identical (the existing golden/probe tests and the digest of the corpus documents); a baked physics cache of a world with voxel bodies is REFUSED with a message that says so (the cache layout has no place for revisions or cuts; no cache of cells yet), and a stale cache cannot serve a voxel document; the sloping-ground / out-of-plane-axis limit is stated again in the ledger entry of this stage.

## Decisions I am taking (say if you disagree)
- Crater axis: a Voxels owner has no analytic surface normal (`surface::normal` is None for it), so the impact's own normal is the axis (`axis_and_closing_speed` already falls back to it); the cut at an impact on a jagged cell surface uses the contact normal of the manifold. Stated untested for sloping ground.
- The cut is instantaneous at the step the impact is reported (one step after the contact).
- A world with cell bodies is not cacheable (W5).
- `fragmentMinCells` and `maxFragments` serve both a crater and a fracture on the owner (VOX11); slots exist only for a crater (a fracture's fragments are the partition's pieces: their number is the partition's, and a partition with more pieces than `maxFragments` is the policy's overflow).
